Rules and controls

You have two minutes to find a low-cost arrangement. Rooks, bishops and queens stop at the first target, obstacle or piece. Knights jump. Kings cover adjacent squares. In this game's original rules, pawns attack all four diagonal neighbours.

Targets are marked with a crosshair; covered targets gain a bright outline. You cannot place pieces on targets or obstacles. Submit a valid solution, then keep improving it until time runs out. The browser edition preserves the original single-player puzzle rules; it does not require a multiplayer server.
