About Me

I'm Ze Jing, a final-year Game Development student at Amsterdam University of Applied Sciences. I like the part of a project where the state has to be right — the window that can't be dragged off-screen, the call that waits for an answer, the message that stays unread until you actually read it.

I completed a three-year MBO programme in one year and graduated with distinction. Right now I'm building Trust Protocol, a desktop-style serious game about bank-impersonation fraud, in TypeScript and Lit.
